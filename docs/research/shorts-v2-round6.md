## Adversarial review and revised script (not rendered)
Version A (the five cards in evidence_pilot.py) is too close to a narrated slide deck: the opening already reveals dumping, and the middle gives only generic market/waste explanation. The script misses the most interesting finding of the paper, which is the economic incentive to use otherwise poor-quality textiles when trading by weight. Reject as editorial pilot; keep the licensed-photo render as an asset pipeline diagnostic. Actual script currently uses VOICEVOX without separate sound accents; no claim of sound-design implementation.
Version B (draft): begin with the actual Fig. 2(c) sign advertising a 45kg bale of jeans for CLP 200,000, a specific observed offer (not a current price or the price of every bale). Then ask why some importers use low-quality clothing to fill bales. The investigation reports that weight-based purchase/sale incentivizes using as much material as possible; do not claim the pictured first-quality bale contains low-quality clothing. Then contrast the market with Fig. 5's dated dump photos. Some clothing is resold/reused and some discarded; no specific garment was tracked. The final picture answers the question without a moralizing line. Need acquire Fig. 2, actual moving footage or physically meaningful action, and audience/market comparison.
