Local render B: same story, first 3 seconds close-up, larger stacks, removed bills physically arc toward a separate tray; later camera and soundtrack retained in principle (not a perfectly isolated A/B; both framing and bill animation changed). Rendered and contact sheet checked. FAIL editorial: machine looks like basic blocks rather than cinematic props, abstract gold pieces not immediately recognizable as money; final pullback is too small and no human emotional stakes, simple conclusion. Audio generated, not listened to. Both videos are local outputs, not uploaded to repo or posted.
No more generic conveyor metaphor refinements until stronger script is selected. Reusable learning: important objects occupy enough of frame, movement must visibly cause the result; a close-up cannot fix a weak premise.
